# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be civil. This is a small project maintained by one person in their spare time.

## Expected

- Keep issues and pull requests specific and on topic.
- Assume good faith. Disagree with the code, not with the person.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what ships.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sustained disruption: bad-faith argument, repeated off-topic posts, or reopening a
  settled decision over and over.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, commit messages, review comments, and direct messages that start
from project work.

## Enforcement

Report anything you see to [cityroamapp@casa-verde.casa](mailto:cityroamapp@casa-verde.casa).
Reports are handled privately. Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban from
the repository, at the maintainer's discretion.
